# PERSONA: early_career — new graduate / 0–2 years experience

This candidate has thin or no professional history. DO NOT score them on work
trajectory they cannot have. Reward potential, education, projects, and trajectory
signals. The single most common failure is penalizing a junior for not being a senior.
Do not do that.

## Allowed formats (for format_recommendation)
- `functional`   → default when work history is essentially empty
- `combination`  → when there are internships/projects worth a short timeline
- `chronological`→ only if they have ~2 years of real, relevant roles
- NEVER recommend `academic` for this persona unless target sector is academic
  (in which case the academic persona should have been selected instead — flag mismatch)

## Weighted dimensions (weights MUST sum to 1.0)

| key                      | label                         | weight |
|--------------------------|-------------------------------|--------|
| education_strength       | Education & academic standing | 0.22   |
| internships_projects     | Internships & projects        | 0.24   |
| skills_relevance         | Skills vs. target role        | 0.20   |
| potential_signals        | Trajectory & initiative       | 0.12   |
| presentation_clarity     | Clarity & structure           | 0.12   |
| ats_keyword_match        | ATS keyword coverage          | 0.10   |

## How to score each dimension

- education_strength: University prestige genuinely matters in the Turkish market —
  reward recognized institutions, strong GPA if shown, relevant coursework, honors.
  Do NOT invent a GPA. Absence of a strong academic signal caps this near 60.
- internships_projects: This is the proxy for experience. Reward concrete internships,
  capstone/graduation projects, hackathons, open-source, freelance. Reward specificity
  (what was built, tools used, outcome) over titles. Empty here is a major weakness.
- skills_relevance: Match declared skills to the target role's real requirements.
  Reward demonstrated skills (used in a project) over merely listed ones.
- potential_signals: Leadership in clubs, volunteering, competitions, self-driven
  learning, certifications. This is where a thin CV earns upside.
- presentation_clarity: Juniors are judged harder on clarity because there's less
  content to hide behind. Reward clean structure, no fluff, one page.
- ats_keyword_match: Provided by the ATS sub-check. Reward presence of role-critical
  terms in both Turkish and English where relevant.

## Persona-specific findings to always check
- data_gap if projects lack any outcome/metric → ask candidate to add result.
- weakness if the CV reads as a list of responsibilities with no initiative signal.
- strength if a project demonstrably maps to the target role's core skill.
