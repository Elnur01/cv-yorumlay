# PERSONA: experienced — mid-career professional / ~3–10 years

The broad middle of the market: white-collar and tech individual contributors and
first-line managers. Scored primarily on QUANTIFIED ACHIEVEMENTS and ATS fit, not on
education (which is now table stakes) and not on potential (they must show results).

## Allowed formats
- `chronological` → default; recruiters expect it and it is most ATS-compatible
- `combination`   → when a specific skill cluster needs spotlighting over the timeline
- `functional`    → only for a candidate with a genuine employment gap or pivot
- NEVER `academic`

## Weighted dimensions (weights MUST sum to 1.0)

| key                      | label                          | weight |
|--------------------------|--------------------------------|--------|
| impact_quantification    | Quantified achievements        | 0.28   |
| role_relevance           | Experience vs. target role     | 0.22   |
| skills_depth             | Depth of relevant skills       | 0.16   |
| progression              | Career progression & scope     | 0.12   |
| ats_keyword_match        | ATS keyword coverage           | 0.12   |
| presentation_clarity     | Clarity & structure            | 0.10   |

## How to score each dimension

- impact_quantification: The decisive dimension. Reward bullets with numbers (%, ₺/$,
  time saved, scale, users, revenue). A CV of responsibilities with no outcomes caps
  near 55 regardless of seniority. If numbers are absent but the role clearly produced
  measurable results, raise a `data_gap`, do not invent figures.
- role_relevance: How directly does the most recent ~5 years map to the target role?
  Reward relevant recency; discount old, off-target roles.
- skills_depth: Reward demonstrated depth (led, owned, architected) over breadth of
  buzzwords. Tool lists with no context score low.
- progression: Reward title/scope growth, increasing team size, budget, or ownership.
  Lateral-only histories are fine but do not earn the top band here.
- ats_keyword_match: From the ATS sub-check. Penalize missing role-critical keywords.
- presentation_clarity: 1–2 pages, reverse-chronological, scannable. Penalize walls of
  text and inconsistent formatting.

## Persona-specific findings to always check
- data_gap on every achievement bullet that describes action but no result.
- weakness if the summary is generic ("hardworking team player") with no specialization.
- format_flag if the CV exceeds 2 pages without executive-level justification.
- strength for any bullet with a credible, role-relevant quantified outcome.
