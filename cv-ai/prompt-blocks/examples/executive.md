# MATCHED EXAMPLE: executive persona

Exactly ONE example is attached per call, matched to the selected persona. This replaces
the old approach of sending all example CVs every time. It calibrates the model on what
"good" looks like for THIS candidate type. The example is illustrative, not a template to
copy verbatim. Never lift its content into the candidate's output. (Identity anonymized —
only the professional substance that calibrates scoring is retained.)

## Example score output (abbreviated, shows the bar)
A senior enterprise-sales leadership CV in this band typically scores UNEVENLY rather
than uniformly high. The two most recent roles carry hard quota and revenue-share figures
(e.g. "grew a channel to over a third of the unit's total revenue against a nine-figure
USD quota") — exactly the business_impact evidence this persona rewards. But several
earlier roles in the same document, despite senior titles ("Senior Manager", "Regional
Sales Manager"), are written as territory and relationship duties with no attached
number. The overall score lands around 68-72: strong on recent scope_of_leadership and
business_impact, weak on strategic_narrative because no role explicitly frames a
before/after transformation, market entry, or turnaround.

## What separates 85 from 68 for this persona
- 85: every senior-title role, not just the most recent one, carries at least one
  quantified outcome (quota, revenue, team size, or territory scope); the summary opens
  with a specific specialization (a named sector or deal type) rather than a general
  "agile seller" framing; stakeholder_breadth is explicit (named counterpart level —
  board, C-suite, or named global vendor relationships) instead of implied by employer
  name alone.
- 68: recent roles are strong, older roles regress to duty-listing; leadership scope
  (team size, quota) is stated for only one or two of many roles; the professional
  summary describes an approach/style rather than a specialization or scale.

## Persona-specific calibration notes drawn from this example
- weakness (major): a personal-interests block (hobbies, family) at the end of an
  executive CV is a presentation_gravitas deduction, not a neutral inclusion — flag for
  removal in rebuild regardless of localization wrapper.
- kvkk_flag: this profile shape (full home address, exact date and place of birth,
  nationality, photo) is typical of a domestic-market executive CV. Under the
  intl_multinational wrapper, flag ALL of these fields for removal. Under tr_domestic,
  flag only the full street address and exact birth date — the photo is not flagged there.
- data_gap: long tenure at globally recognized employers is a domain_authority signal on
  its own, but should only score highly when at least one attached number confirms scale
  (quota, team size, revenue) rather than resting on employer name alone.
- format_flag: a CV spanning this many roles and years is appropriate LENGTH for the
  executive persona — do not recommend cutting older roles entirely. Recommend compressing
  them to one outcome-bearing line each instead.
