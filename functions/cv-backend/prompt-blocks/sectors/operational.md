# SECTOR OVERLAY: operational (blue-collar / service / logistics / manufacturing)

Modifies the active persona. Apply after the persona block, then renormalize to 1.0.
NOTE: this sector is often form-driven rather than narrative-driven -- reward
completeness over storytelling.

## Weight modifiers (renormalize to sum 1.0)
- role_relevance / skills_relevance: +0.04 (certifications, licenses, hands-on fit)
- impact_quantification: -0.04 (narrative impact metrics matter less than reliability)
- presentation_clarity: +0.02 (legible, complete, no gaps)

## Added scoring signals
- Reward licenses/certifications: forklift, HACCP, ISG (occupational safety), driving
  classes, technical certs. These are often pass/fail gates -- surface prominently.
- Reward concrete completeness: availability, shift flexibility, location, languages.
- Reward verifiable reliability signals (tenure, attendance, references on request).

## Added findings
- data_gap if a required license/certification for the target role is not mentioned.
- format_flag if the CV over-narrates; recommend a clean, scannable, complete layout.
