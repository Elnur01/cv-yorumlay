# PERSONA: executive — senior leadership / director / C-level

This candidate is hired for judgment, scope, and business outcomes — not task lists.
A 1-page resume actively HURTS them; do not penalize length up to ~3 pages when the
content earns it. Reward P&L ownership, scale of responsibility, and strategic outcomes.

## Allowed formats
- `combination`   → default; lead with a leadership profile, then a senior timeline
- `chronological` → acceptable for a clean upward trajectory
- NEVER `functional` (reads as hiding something at this level)
- NEVER `academic` unless target is academic leadership (then academic persona applies)

## Weighted dimensions (weights MUST sum to 1.0)

| key                      | label                              | weight |
|--------------------------|------------------------------------|--------|
| business_impact          | P&L / business outcomes            | 0.28   |
| scope_of_leadership      | Team size, budget, org scope       | 0.22   |
| strategic_narrative      | Strategy & transformation evidence | 0.18   |
| domain_authority         | Sector authority & board exposure  | 0.12   |
| stakeholder_breadth      | Cross-functional / external scope  | 0.10   |
| presentation_gravitas    | Executive clarity & restraint      | 0.10   |

## How to score each dimension

- business_impact: Reward outcomes at the business level — revenue grown, cost taken
  out, margin, market entry, turnaround, valuation, exits. Numbers expected here; their
  absence at this level is a major weakness, not a data_gap to excuse.
- scope_of_leadership: Reward explicit scale — people managed (direct + indirect),
  budget/P&L owned, geographies, business units. Vague "led teams" scores low.
- strategic_narrative: Reward evidence of setting direction, not just executing —
  transformations, new functions built, M&A, restructures. Reward before/after framing.
- domain_authority: Sector depth, board/advisory roles, recognized external standing.
- stakeholder_breadth: C-suite peers, board, investors, regulators, key customers.
- presentation_gravitas: At this level, restraint signals seniority. Penalize
  junior-style task bullets; reward concise outcome statements. Length is fine if earned.

## Persona-specific findings to always check
- weakness (major) if leadership scope is asserted but never quantified.
- weakness if bullets describe operational tasks a manager two levels down would write.
- strength for any clear, attributable business-level outcome with scale.
- format_flag only if length is padded with low-level detail, not for length itself.
