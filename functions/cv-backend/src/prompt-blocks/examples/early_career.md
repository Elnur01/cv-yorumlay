# MATCHED EXAMPLE: early_career persona

Exactly ONE example is attached per call, matched to the selected persona. This replaces
the old approach of sending all example CVs every time. It calibrates the model on what
"good" looks like for THIS candidate type. The example is illustrative, not a template to
copy verbatim. Never lift its content into the candidate's output. (Identity anonymized —
only the professional substance that calibrates scoring is retained.)

## Example score output (abbreviated, shows the bar)
A strong early_career-persona CV scores ~88-90 overall: the candidate is a senior
engineering student at a well-regarded technical university who already holds a part-time
professional role in their target field (not just an internship), and has progressed
within a large student-run technical team from an individual contributor to a leadership
role overseeing multiple sub-teams and dozens of members. A concrete, nationally funded
research project and documented international competition placements provide quantified
potential signals. Skills are demonstrated, not merely listed — e.g. a specific tool stack
tied to a completed internship deliverable (a working inventory-tracking tool), not just a
skills line. Multilingual competence adds transferable-skill breadth.

## What separates 88 from 60 for this persona
- 88: the current role, even if junior, is already IN the target field; leadership scope
  is quantified (team size, number of sub-groups managed); at least one funded or
  competitively recognized project exists; the skills list reappears inside project
  bullets, not just as a floating list.
- 60: same educational tier, but the experience section is internship titles with duty
  descriptions and no scale or outcome ("assisted with X"); no project names a funding
  source or result; skills are listed once and never demonstrated; no leadership or
  initiative signal beyond coursework.

## Persona-specific calibration notes drawn from this example
- data_gap: even a strong CV like this can be missing the *outcome* of an in-progress
  funded project — an ongoing project should still name an interim result or expected
  deliverable if one exists, rather than being left open-ended.
- strength: a promotion within a single extracurricular team (specialist -> group leader)
  is a genuine progression signal for this persona and should be surfaced explicitly in
  rebuild, not buried inside a bullet.
- format_flag: a professional-sounding job title held concurrently with full-time study is
  common in this persona and should carry precise dates (start date, "Present") so it
  reads as current, real experience rather than an ambiguous or future role.
